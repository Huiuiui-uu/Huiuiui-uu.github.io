Upon their decision to host Stan within their premises, MPX Gallery had no intention of presenting it within a conventional, natural history museum-style setting. JAAX took the initiative to create an immersive environment implementing AR technology. 

This immersive space was designed to replicate the visual and auditory ambiance of the era in which Tyrannosauruses once roamed- strategic placement of mirrors and LED screens was employed, providing viewers with a 360-degree perspective of Stan, as well as the three-dimensional projection of its skin on Stan’s skeleton from certain view angles.
