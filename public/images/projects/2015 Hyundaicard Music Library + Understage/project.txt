One of Hyundaicard’s series of libraries around Seoul, Music Library features about 15,000 items on music, of which more than two-thirds are vinyl records. Below Music Library is a cafe, practice studios for bands, and a high-end concert hall that can host up to 500 standing guests. The building features work by renowned artists such as Vhils, JR and Alex Prager.
