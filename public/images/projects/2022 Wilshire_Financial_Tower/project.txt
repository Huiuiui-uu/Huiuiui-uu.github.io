A classic Mid-Wilshire tower in Los Angeles is undergoing a multi-phase gut renovation. Phase I involves the facade renewal of the tower and car park, along with the renovation of the canopy and entry lobby. Future phases will include a new basement speakeasy, a rooftop bar & restaurant, as well as the interior renovation of all office spaces.

Collaboration with OFKA.
