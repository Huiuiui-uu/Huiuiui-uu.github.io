Nestled amidst the picturesque mountains of Cherry Valley in Southern California, this 30-acre site will serve as the idyllic setting for a Catholic retreat village. The village will encompass a chapel, dormitories, and versatile event spaces.

Emphasizing a commitment to nature, the majority of the site will be allocated to cultivating gardens and farms that showcase local flora and vegetables. The site will feature multiple serene meditation and prayer areas, both indoors and in the great outdoors. This project is currently in the masterplan / entitlement phase.
