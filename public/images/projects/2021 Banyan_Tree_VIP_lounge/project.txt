Banyan Tree's VIP preview and sales gallery for its Osiria condominiums draw inspiration from the historical significance of the region, particularly its association with sambe production. The design incorporates elements from both the fabric itself and the geometric patterns of the loom, creating a space that pays homage to this cultural heritage.
