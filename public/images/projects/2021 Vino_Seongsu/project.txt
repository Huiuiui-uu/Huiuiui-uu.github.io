A gut renovation of a leatherwork factory in what is now the trendiest neighborhood of Seoul, Vino Seongsu features a wine shop and a restaurant on the ground floor, and a large cellar with tasting rooms in the basement. The building's parking lot currently serves as the site for Dior Seongsu.
