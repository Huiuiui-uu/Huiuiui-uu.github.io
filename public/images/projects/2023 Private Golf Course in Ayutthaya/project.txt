Although his golf course is already among the most prestigious and exclusive clubs in Asia, our client envisioned taking it to an even higher echelon by introducing additional programs and enhancing its amenities.

JAAX has been tasked with scrutinizing the site and conceptualizing the programming and design for this expansion. While the client has outlined specific requests for 30 new standalone villas and a second clubhouse, we proposed programs encompassing an art walk, a gallery for his art collection and a bird sanctuary on the bird island, as well as a 120-key boutique hotel.
