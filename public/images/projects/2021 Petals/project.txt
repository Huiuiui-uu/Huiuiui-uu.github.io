A floating kindergarten above an existing garden on a slope, in Berlin. The building is imagined to be a series of tree houses where classrooms, play rooms and nap rooms area sprinkled along a continuous pathway of steps, ramps and slides. These trees are placed along the perimeter of the site, internalizing a courtyard that allows for not only ample natural light in the spaces, but a physical and visual connection to the garden and playground below.
